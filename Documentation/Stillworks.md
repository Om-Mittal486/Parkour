# The Stillworks

An abandoned utility city organised around an enormous hollow concrete core. Its original purpose is deliberately unresolved: intake streets become thermal infrastructure, vehicle circulation becomes construction, and an institutional complex gives way to a communications crown. The climb ends at 588 metres; the last mast reaches approximately 675 metres.

## Play

Open `Assets/Scenes/Stillworks.unity`, press Play, and choose **Begin Ascent** on the title screen. This is the first enabled scene in Build Settings. `MainScene.unity` remains the original controller test scene.

WASD moves; Shift toggles sprint; Space jumps; Ctrl slides; E collects the laundry at the Crown. The objective is to bring the clothes back to the starting street shelter before the rain soaks them. Wetness rises from zero to 100% over 45 minutes of active play, including the trip down. Reaching the roof alone does not win. Escape pauses movement and wetness, releases the cursor, and offers restart, settings and save-and-return. The run starts on movement. Successful delivery records the best complete errand time locally. Falling below the foundation returns the player to the street without clearing the timer. There are no checkpoint rooms or automatic upward teleports.

Fifty-six original dry-humoured narrator lines accompany the errand. Physical trigger volumes mark the thirteen subsequent circulation-level arrivals and two gallery landmarks on each of fourteen levels. For example, entering the second level queues its specific arrival line. Visited cues play once; queued speech waits for the current line to finish. Story events also cover laundry collection, descent landmarks, 75% wetness, delivery and failure. Six repeatable fall taunts rotate when the player loses height, with a six-second cooldown. Baked Microsoft David voice clips accompany subtitles. Both the collected-laundry state and the expanded 64-bit played-cue mask are saved, preserving original cue indices for old saves. The dialogue source is `Assets/Stillworks/Dialogue.json`; `Tools/GenerateNarration.ps1` regenerates offline clips. The deadline is editable through `StillworksSession.soakSeconds` for future balance passes.

The native Canvas interface includes title, pause, settings, HUD and summit screens. Settings control sensitivity, FOV, master/music/rain volumes, camera motion, inverted look, borderless fullscreen and economy graphics. Buttons and toggles have soft click feedback. A suspended run stores its current timer and latest clear standing position, with an automatic save every 15 seconds. Resuming checks that position against the map before loading it. Only one suspended run is retained; beginning a new run replaces it. Original wind, concrete footsteps, landings, slide scrape and a 64-second ambient music loop require no external services. `Tools/GenerateSoundtrack.ps1` regenerates the baked soundtrack. Music ducks during narration; rain is quieter indoors.

## Architectural route

| Elevation | District | Circulation and identity |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–84 m | Intake Quarter | Streets, sealed service doors, dead lamps, abandoned cars, wide terraces and processional stairs. |
| 84–168 m | Heat Exchange | Reservoirs, pipe mains, covered process galleries, broken expansion joints and loading lintels. |
| 168–252 m | Transit Stack | Wide asphalt parking floors, column arcades, divided bays, long graded ramps and a disused parking annex. |
| 252–336 m | Unfinished Works | Narrower construction floors, exposed columns, projecting rebar, steel roof girders and a lattice tower crane. |
| 336–420 m | The Ministry | Deep concrete fins, tall sheltered halls, monumental walls, courtyards and a blind administrative tower. |
| 420–504 m | Relay Needles | Exposed maintenance galleries, vertical cable risers, longer broken joints and an elevated relay crane. |
| 504–588 m | The Crown | Concrete blades, narrow upper circulation, a final bridge and a large observation roof beneath a communications arch. |

The fourteen ascending circulation levels use six irregular building wings each. Wings are joined by broad courts rather than isolated landing blocks. Width, enclosure, surface, facade rhythm and structural vocabulary change by district. Massive plinths, cantilever knees, deep wall sections and the continuous central core explain the structure. Lower roofs remain visible, and fog starts at 700 metres rather than obscuring the climb.

The main route follows floors, ramps, staircases and fractured gallery joints. Each district also has an exposed maintenance stair with missing landing grating. It climbs 42 metres and skips an entire circulation level. Lower maintenance stairs have five 3.8 metre gaps; upper stairs use 6.4 metre gaps and 85 cm landing strips. Twelve 65 cm transfer girders in Unfinished Works and Relay Needles demand precise steering, with maintenance catch roofs six metres below. Side courts branch from the main floors and rejoin them. Broken main galleries have catch floors five metres down with side ramps returning to the upper floor.

Main-floor openings have 3.2 metre gaps in the middle districts and 4.2 metre gaps high up. Seeded variation changes hazard types, positions, dimensions, skew, handedness, combinations and pacing without regenerating routes during play. Hazards include concrete dividers, skewed girders, pipe bundles, toppled cabinets, compressor slaloms, raised inspection beds, offset loading shutters and abandoned shuttering. Sixty-two low passages vary between lintels, round service mains, roller shutters and ventilation hoods; some galleries remain open for sprinting. Physical see-through guard fences close required lanes; low roofs prevent jumping over slide obstacles. Recovery and exploration joins remain usable.

Wall-running requires holding Space while airborne alongside a facade with forward momentum above 8 m/s. Releasing Space kicks away and upward once, with a short separation cooldown. Runs last up to 1.65 seconds with a slow descent; landing refreshes the same wall. Holding Ctrl while falling against a side or front wall caps descent at 3.5 m/s; pressing Space kicks out of the slide. Twenty-four broken galleries include a recorded wall crossing. Wall moves are optional alternatives to ordinary sprint jumps. Vaulting remains a future controller feature.

The global atmosphere profile uses ACES tone mapping, cool shadows, warm highlights, stronger contrast, subtle bloom and fine grain. Gameplay and title cameras both use it. Blue facade panels, oxide-red steelwork, teal machinery, ochre hazard markings, painted fences, abandoned cars and coloured laundry break up the concrete palette. Speed adds mild edge darkening, a small FOV increase and restrained chromatic separation. Wall-running leans the camera toward the corrected side; landings, slides and wall contact emit a small pool of concrete dust. Reduced motion disables lean, speed FOV, chromatic separation and dust particles; economy graphics omit ambient motes. Long sightlines remain clear.

The sky is an original procedural cloud shader with broad drifting cloud banks, fine upper wisps, a warm horizon and a softly obscured sun aligned with the directional light. Flood-retention reservoirs surround the foundation, with restrained animated water ripples, weathered dividing walls, sparse reeds and moss. Eighteen distant utility halls and six exhaust stacks extend the skyline. These surroundings sit outside the climb and retain the four dry floodplain return ramps.

Rain uses transparent streaks in three 360-degree bands around the active camera, reaching 220 metres, plus a separate 720-metre-wide city volume from the foundation to 700 metres. The four pools cap at 22,200 drops; a 384-particle pool draws small expanding splashes on exposed floors. Covered emission points are skipped; raycasts along the wind direction end drops at roofs/floors. Local pools clear after large camera teleports. Economy graphics reduce emission in every band. This is bounded visual weather rather than a fluid simulation.

The route length and a theoretical uninterrupted sprint time are recorded in `Stillworks-validation.txt`. The intended 15–30 minute skilled run is a design target, not a measured human completion time. The route can be substantially shortened through the maintenance stairs. Human playtesting should tune recognition, fatigue, missed-jump recovery and repeated-run timing before treating it as production-balanced.

## Unity implementation

`Stillworks / Build playable map` rebuilds the separate scene using the original scene's existing player and input references. It writes stable reusable material and mesh assets under `Assets/Stillworks/Generated`. Rebuilding replaces generated scene edits; keep bespoke edits in another scene or change the generator. The route manifest records passage endpoints and widths for inspection and validation.

Architecture is generated in the editor and saved as normal scene objects. There is no runtime map generation, package download or dependence on Blender. Structural geometry is combined by district wing and shared material. Secondary dressing uses LOD groups; simplified city blocks carry distant silhouettes. Boxes provide most collision, ramps use small wedge meshes, and decorative nosings use smooth staircase collision. Four native-generated seamless 512px surface maps supply concrete aggregate, asphalt fractures, corrosion and chipped paint. Triplanar world-space sampling keeps texel scale consistent on enormous walls; mipmaps and anisotropic filtering reduce shimmer. A subtle height channel supplies surface relief. Large facade paint bands and service stencils add variation. The foundation sits 18 cm below the main starting floor, junction courts sit 5 cm below circulation floors, and the maintenance entrance begins 3.5 cm above the floor to avoid overlapping coplanar materials.

The new scene normalises the player scale, removes its redundant capsule collider and visible body mesh, sets a 1.62 metre eye height and a 2.4 kilometre far clip, and retains the serialized 14 m/s sprint speed. The movement change prevents standing into low ceilings, permits exiting a passage after slide momentum expires, restores full height after a slide, and exposes motion reset for restarting a run.

The generator's `Validate current map` command uses Unity physics to check recorded floor continuity, capsule headroom and conservative jump distances. A geometric check is not a substitute for a full player-driven run or target-hardware profiling. The level remains an editable gameplay environment intended for those next production passes.
